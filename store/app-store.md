## Notes pour la relecture d’Apple (« App Review Information »)

```
Toki Quest is a TOEIC preparation app. No account is needed to use it.

To review the full content without buying: sign in with the demo account below (Profile > Sign in). It has the full plan.
Demo account: <e-mail of a demo account> / <password> — create it on the server, then run: npm run grant -- <e-mail>

To review the in-app purchase: Profile > See the full plan (or tap any locked unit). The one-time, non-consumable product "toki_quest_full" is the only way to pay in the app; "Restore my purchases" is on the same screen.

Microphone and speech recognition are used only in Speaking exercises (Path > Speaking and pronunciation), after an in-app explanation and consent. Audio is never stored or sent to our servers.

Accounts can be deleted in the app: Profile > Delete my account.
```
